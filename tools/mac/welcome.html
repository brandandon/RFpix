RFpix @VERSION@

Digital (HamDRM) and analog SSTV pictures over ham radio.

RFpix is installed in your Applications folder. If an older RFpix is there, it's replaced (it's closed first if it's running). Your settings and pictures are kept.

The first time RFpix listens to your radio, macOS asks whether it may use the microphone: click Allow (the radio's sound input counts as a microphone).
